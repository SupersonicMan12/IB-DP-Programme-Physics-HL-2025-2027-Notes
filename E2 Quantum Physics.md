Thesis: **Light has particle nature, while matter has wave nature.**

### Photoelectric Effect: Particle Nature of Light

***Photoelectric effect***: electrons are emitted from the surface of a metal when electromagnetic radiation above a certain frequency was directed at the metal.

![[Screenshot 2026-06-12 at 18.57.51.png]]
The Photoelectric Effect Experiment: light shines on the emitter plate, causing electrons to be ejected by the photoelectric effect, and these electrons travel to the other collector plate.

Classical wave theory could not explain why photoelectrons are emitted only above a threshold frequency, why their maximum kinetic energy depends on frequency rather than intensity, and why emission is almost instantaneous even at low intensity.

Einstein's explanation: light comes in photons, with $E = hf$, then if the photon energy exceeds the metal's work function $\phi$, the extra energy becomes kinetic energy: $$E_{max} = hf-\phi,$$ while increasing intensity only increases the number of emitted electrons, not their individual KE.

 ![[Screenshot 2026-06-12 at 19.04.10.png]]
Now notice the voltage, when sufficiently large $V_s$ stopping voltage is applied, $$eV_s = E_{max},$$ even the fastest electrons fail to escape. 

### Compton Scattering: Particle Nature of Light

Compton Scattering = the change in the wavelength of the incident and scattered photon.

![[Screenshot 2026-06-12 at 19.11.19.png]]
X-rays were fired at a carbon target, the scattered wave had a longer wavelength than the incident wave: $$\lambda_f - \lambda_i = \Delta \lambda = \frac{h}{m_ec}(1-\cos{\theta}) = 2.4\times10^{-12} \times (1-\cos{\theta}).$$
### Wave Nature of Matter
