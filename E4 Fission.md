Nuclear fission = a large nucleus splitting into two or more smaller nuclei.

### Neutron-induced fission and chain reaction

Energy is released in the spontaneous and nucleus-induced fission:
$${}^{1}_{0}n + {}^{235}_{92}U \rightarrow {}^{236}_{92}U^* \rightarrow {}^{141}_{56}Ba + {}^{92}_{36}Kr + 3{}^{1}_{0}n + \text{energy}$$

![[Screenshot 2026-06-12 at 20.52.02.png]]
Because the binding energy per nucleon decreases, the total binding energy decreases, with extra energy being released as kinetic energy.

This reaction can become a chain reaction: self-sustaining. For this to happen, the concentration of U235 must be sufficiently high. We do fuel enrichment: the critical mass is the minimum amount of the fissile isotope needed to sustain a nuclear chain reaction.

Spontaneous Fission (not induced by a nucleon) occurs for unstable isotopes: $${}^{239}_{94}\mathrm{Pu}
\rightarrow
{}^{112}_{46}\mathrm{Pd}
+
{}^{124}_{48}\mathrm{Cd}
+
3{}^{1}_{0}\mathrm{n}.$$
### Nuclear Reactor:

![[Screenshot 2026-06-12 at 21.14.22.png]]

Power plants in general use steam to turn a turbine. The core is where the nuclear reaction happens.

- Nuclear fuel provides the nuclear potential energy
- Control rods are inserted (raised or lowered) to absorb some neutrons and slow or stop the chain reaction as needed
- Moderators (deuterium in the form of heavy water or graphite) are substances mixed in to slow down the neutrons to increase fission probability, so that U235 can absorb the neutron
- Heat exchangers transfer energy to a secondary water system, creating steam...
- Shielding of concrete and steel is used to protect the workers and the environment
	- This is what IB cares most about: ethics, the environment, and peoples' well-being :)

Products of Nuclear Fission
- Products are often highly radioactive isotopes, which emit ionizing radiation harmful to human health and the environment
	- Low-level waste (LLW) - disposed safely in specific landfills with labelling.
	- High-level waste (HLW) must be stored in special long-term storage facilities in deep geological repositories
